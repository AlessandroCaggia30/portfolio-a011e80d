ABM Firm — Model & Results

Assumptions · equations · results on effort, wages, profits, cooperation.

Assumptions

Agents

  • Workers are risk-averse with CARA utility, private effort choice, additive Gaussian noise on output. Holmström & Milgrom 1987 §I
  • Effort cost is quadratic: $c(e) = \tfrac{1}{2} k e^2$. Holmström 1979
  • Ability $\alpha_i$ is heterogeneous, drawn from $\mathcal{N}(1, \sigma_\alpha^2)$ and fixed across periods. Holmström 1982 career concerns
  • Reciprocity: workers raise effort when the wage exceeds a reference wage. Fehr & Falk 2002

Firm

  • Risk-neutral principal offering a contract $\{w_0, \beta\}$; profits $\pi = g\bar e - w_{\text{bill}}$.
  • Contract space: A0 flat wage, A1 linear piece rate, A2 tournament, A3 efficiency wage, A4 team pay + peer pressure, A5 back-loaded, A6 career concerns, A7 subjective bonus, A8 equity.

Environment

  • Peer network: Watts–Strogatz small-world, rewiring prob. 0.15. Watts & Strogatz 1998
  • Supervisor rating adds noise $\varepsilon_m \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_m^2)$ for A2/A3/A7 contracts. MacLeod 2003
  • Supervisor–worker collusion is available at cost $\kappa$; audit probability $p$, penalty pen. Tirole 1986
